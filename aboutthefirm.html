About Our Firm

A premier audit, tax, and advisory partnership serving organizations across Kenya and the East & Central African region.

Who We Are

Institutional Financial Integrity Rooted in African Excellence

Founded in 2010, Mwangi Wangondu & Associates is a registered firm of Certified Public Accountants that offers Audit Assurance and Financial Capacity Development Services with over 15 years of operational experience across several countries in Africa.

The firm has undertaken high-impact engagements in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia, and Rwanda. We maintain a multidisciplinary pool of professionals with unmatched knowledge of formulating homegrown solutions to emerging operational challenges in a sustainable manner.

Our team draws from long-standing experience in both local and regional business environments, statutory regulations, and custom legal requirements, while anchoring every audit on international standards and best practices.

"We take distinct pride in our senior team’s specialized service delivery to public entities, state corporations, government institutions, Cooperative Societies, private firms, public development programmes, and the international NGO sector."
