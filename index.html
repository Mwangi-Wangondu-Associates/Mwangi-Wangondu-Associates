About the Firm

Decades of Collective Insight & Uncompromising Standards

Founded in 2010, Mwangi Wangondu & Associates is a premier audit and advisory firm serving clients across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia, and Rwanda. We are a registered firm of Certified Public Accountants offering comprehensive audit, assurance, and financial capacity development services.

Our multidisciplinary team of experts combines deep knowledge of local business environments, statutory mandates, and custom procedures with international standards and best practices. We hold an established distinction in service delivery to public entities, private firms, cooperative societies, donor-funded programmes, and the NGO sector.
